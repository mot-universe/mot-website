We Have a Thing for a Good Fight.

We like fights where the odds aren't always in your favor, but a well-timed decision can turn everything around.

That's why we spend time exploring how attack flow, how enemies respond, and how the smallest interactions can change the feel of a fight. We build, test, and refine these ideas through gameplay experiments.
