about

Independent Studio. Established Experience. Based in Malang.

MOT is a game development and interactive production studio based in Malang, Indonesia, with commercial project Experience since 2018.
Surrounded by East Java's remarkable landscapes, Malang is also one of Indonesia's established education hubs, home to strong pipeline of emerging IT, engineering, and creative talent.
This combination of technical talent and creative culture provides a strong foundation for our work, from commercial productions to immersive attractions and connected systems.
